Module 05 // Advanced Stochastic Modelling

Monte Carlo Framework

Monte Carlo (MC) simulation is a cornerstone of stochastic computational modelling. While a deterministic model yields a single, unique outcome from fixed inputs, a stochastic model uses pseudo-random sampling from probability distributions to simulate thousands of hypothetical futures.

MC5 Implementation

A trading performance engine like MC5 uses Monte Carlo simulation to break historical performance down into atomic parameters and resamples them to stress-test the strategy under thousands of alternate market conditions.

Critical Risk Metrics:
  • Risk of Ruin: Probability of hitting liquidation before profit targets.
  • Max Drawdown Distributions: 95th/99th percentile of potential equity drops.
  • Wealth Expectancy: Range of final account balances.
Mathematical Paradigms:
  • Bootstrapping: Trade-level resampling with replacement.
  • GBM (Brownian Motion): Asset price path simulation via SDE.
